Passport Run

Privacy policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Updated 4 October 2026: the game now shows ads.

Short version: Passport Run itself does not collect personal data, and your progress stays on your device. The game shows non-personalized ads from Google AdMob, which receives basic device information to show them. We never ask to track you. Kids Mode never shows ads, and a one-time purchase removes them.

What stays on your device

Your passport stamps, scores, settings, travel room and other progress are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. The game also keeps a small local log of game events, such as starting or finishing a country, to show your own history and stats. None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads

After every few retries, a full-screen ad from Google AdMob plays at the results screen, never during play. Ads are requested as non-personalized: they are not based on your interests or other apps, and the game never shows Apple's "Allow tracking?" request. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK receives information such as your device type, iOS version, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with the ad. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the EU, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before any ad. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Kids Mode never shows ads. Remove Ads (Settings) is a one-time purchase that turns them off.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or names required. A nickname you type stays on your device.
  • No personalized ads and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics services. Google AdMob, above, is the only third-party SDK that receives data.
  • No precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Things you choose to share

When you tap a share or save button (a picture of your travel room, an album page, a journey movie, a challenge link, or a passport backup), iOS opens its standard share sheet. You decide where it goes, for example Messages or Photos. The game only uses Photos to add the picture you asked to save. It cannot see your photo library.

Purchases

The optional destination packs, Traveler Pack and Remove Ads are bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and gives the game only a confirmation of which packs you own. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Kids Mode never shows ads and has no sharing buttons. Purchases and sharing elsewhere in the game sit behind a question meant for adults.

Changes

If a future version adds other online features, such as leaderboards, this policy will be updated before that version is released to say exactly what is sent and why.
