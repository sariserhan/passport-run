# Kids country facts

Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-02. Facts are short travel observations, with no claims about cognitive health or performance. They appear after Kids country completion and in the collected sticker album. The album derives ownership from persistent passport discoveries, so old saves receive their earned stickers without a schema migration.

Artwork is original generated scenery with Godot rendering, not photographs, branded assets, accurate maps, or downloaded national symbols. The World Explorer badge now requires all 197 destinations.

Expanded destinations use capital labels from the bundled [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries) snapshot, with a subregion sentence where no capital is supplied. Multiple capital entries are shown together. These are snapshot data, not a live geopolitical feed. License, retrieval date, source hash, filtering, and update instructions are in [destination notes](destinations.md) and `resources/geography/`. The original five landmark facts above remain unchanged.
