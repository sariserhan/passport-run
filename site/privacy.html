Passport Run

Privacy policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Short version: Passport Run does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game works offline, and everything it saves stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your passport stamps, scores, settings, travel room and other progress are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. The game also keeps a small local log of game events, such as starting or finishing a country, to show your own history and stats. None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or names required. A nickname you type stays on your device.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics services, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Things you choose to share

When you tap a share or save button (a picture of your travel room, an album page, a journey movie, a challenge link, or a passport backup), iOS opens its standard share sheet. You decide where it goes, for example Messages or Photos. The game only uses Photos to add the picture you asked to save. It cannot see your photo library.

Purchases

The optional destination packs are bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and gives the game only a confirmation of which packs you own. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Kids Mode, like the rest of the game, collects no data and has no sharing buttons. Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children.

Changes

If a future version adds online features such as leaderboards, this policy will be updated to say exactly what is sent and why, before that version is released.
