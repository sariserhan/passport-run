Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

How do I play?

Watch the path while the safe stones are shown. When they hide, tap a stone in the next row. Each row gives you ten seconds. Reach the far side to stamp your passport and travel on.

I failed a country. Do I start over?

No. On World Tour you retry the same country, and Continue on the main menu picks up at the first country you haven't cleared.

I bought a destination pack but it's locked

Open the pack's page in the game and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. Each pack unlocks only its own destinations.

Why do I see ads, and how do I remove them?

An ad plays at the results screen after every fourth retry; it never interrupts play, and Kids Mode never shows ads. Settings → Remove ads is a one-time purchase that turns them off. If you bought it before, tap Restore purchase there.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Go to New journeys & workshop → Offline passport backup. Tap Create & copy backup, or Share / open backup file to save it somewhere safe such as Files. On the new phone, open the same screen, tap Paste backup, then Preview backup and Restore previewed backup. Your previous save is kept, so you can undo it.

Can I turn off motion, sound or vibration?

Yes. Settings has music and sound volume, reduced motion, a high-contrast path preview, vibration and larger balloon controls. New journeys & workshop → Accessibility adds text size and larger memory-path controls.
